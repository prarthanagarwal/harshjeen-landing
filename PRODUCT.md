# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People discovering HarshJeen InfoTech, its independently maintained products, and the founder's work.

## Product Purpose

Introduce HarshJeen InfoTech as the parent company behind apps and websites built by the founder. Help visitors understand that relationship and move to the relevant product or founder site.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, clear, and quietly ambitious. The experience should feel simple and considered while leaving room for future work.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded corporate templates, inflated claims, and content that duplicates the independently maintained product and founder websites.

## Design Principles

- Make the parent-company relationship immediately clear.
- Show real work through links and screenshots.
- Keep each product and the founder's portfolio independent.
- Use restrained, purposeful color and readable contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text readable, links descriptive, and motion respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
